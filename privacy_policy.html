Robyn - Early Access Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2025

1. Introduction & Your Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to Robyn, your memory sidekick for early parenthood. We understand the deeply personal and sensitive nature of the memories you wish to preserve. This Privacy Policy explains how Robyn (operated by [Your Name/Company Name], "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information during this Early Access Program. Our commitment is to ensure your privacy, warmth, and simplicity in using Robyn, aligning with our vision for an emotionally intelligent memory assistant.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve Robyn's service. The types of information we collect include:

  • Information You Provide Directly:
    • Contact & Account Information: Your email address, collected via our landing page for access to the Early Access Program.
    • Baby Information: Your baby's name and birthday, which you provide during the initial onboarding process to help us deliver age-relevant prompts.
    • Memory Content (Your Input): This includes text messages and voice notes that you send to Robyn via Telegram in response to our prompts. This is the core data we collect to help you preserve memories.
    • AI-Generated Journal Entries: The structured, narrative journal entries that Robyn creates based on your memory content submissions.
  • Information Collected Automatically (Lean Approach):
    • Usage Data: We collect basic data on how you interact with Robyn, such as the date and time of your interactions, the number of prompts you respond to, and the length of your voice notes. This helps us understand engagement and improve the service.
    • Technical Data: Limited technical information about your device and connection (e.g., operating system type) may be collected by our third-party service providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Improve Robyn's Core Service:
    • To deliver personalized, age-relevant weekly prompts to you via Telegram.
    • To transform your text messages and voice notes into timestamped journal entries.
    • To store, organize, and display your journal entries chronologically for your access (currently managed on our backend, with future access via a dedicated app).
  • For Product Validation & Development (Early Access Specific):
    • To validate our core assumptions about parent needs, engagement, and the value of AI-generated memories in a lean environment.
    • AI Model Improvement: We may use anonymized and aggregated versions of your memory inputs to improve the accuracy and "emotionally intelligent" aspects of Robyn's underlying AI models and prompt engineering for future iterations of the service. Your individual, identifiable memories will not be used for this purpose without further explicit consent.
    • To analyze user behavior and engagement patterns to inform future product features and strategic decisions.
  • For Communication:
    • To send you weekly memory prompts and your generated journal entries via Telegram.
    • To send you important updates, feedback requests (e.g., via Google Forms), or announcements related to the Early Access Program.
  • For Safety and Security:
    • To protect Robyn's service and its users from fraudulent, harmful, or unauthorized activities.

4. How We Share Your Information

For this Early Access Program, we share your information with trusted third-party service providers essential for Robyn's operation. We do not sell your personal data.

  • Service Providers: We share necessary information with:
    • Telegram: For sending and receiving messages.
    • Make.com: Our automation platform, which orchestrates the flow of messages and data between services.
    • Airtable: Our database, where your user information, original inputs, and generated journal entries are securely stored.
    • Gemini: During this Early Access phase, your raw memory content is manually fed into Gemini by our team to generate your journal entries (human-in-the-loop "Wizard of Oz" approach). Gemini's policies regarding data usage apply to its services.
  • When Required by Law: We may disclose your information if legally required by a valid court order, governmental request, or applicable law.
  • With Your Explicit Consent: For instance, if you choose to share a specific journal entry via a shareable link, you are consenting to that specific sharing activity.

5. Data Retention

We retain your memory content, journal entries, and associated user data for the duration of your participation in the Early Access Program. Following your departure from the program or its conclusion, we will retain your data for a period of [e.g., 90 days / 6 months] for product improvement and analysis, after which it will be [e.g., anonymized / securely deleted], unless you request earlier deletion.

6. Your Choices & Rights

As a user of Robyn's Early Access Program, you have certain rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Correction: You can request access to the personal data we hold about you and request corrections if it is inaccurate.
  • Deletion: You can request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out of Program: You can opt out of the Early Access Program at any time by contacting us, which will stop new prompts and subsequent data collection.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at robynmemoryapp@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures with our service providers to protect your information, including data encryption in transit and at rest. While we strive to protect your data, no system is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Robyn is intended for use by parents and legal guardians, not directly by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Early Access Program evolves. We will notify you of any significant changes by email or via a message through Robyn's Telegram bot.
